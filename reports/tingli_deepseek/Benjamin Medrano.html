Action Steps

  1. Learn documentation basics: write clear instructions and ticket notes using free resources like Google's Technical Writing course.
  2. Gain help-desk experience: volunteer for IT support at a local nonprofit or use a free help-desk simulation like Spiceworks.
  3. Build computer literacy: take a free online course like 'Computer Basics' from GCFGlobal or Microsoft.
  4. Explore desktop support: practice with virtual labs on platforms like TestOut or ITProTV (free trials).
  5. Develop diagnostic skills: use free tools like Windows Event Viewer or Linux logs, and take a free CompTIA A+ prep course.
